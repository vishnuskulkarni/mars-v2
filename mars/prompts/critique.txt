Severity guide:
- high  = a flaw that, unaddressed, defeats the direction (e.g. the data cannot
          support the claim; an uncontrolled confound; already-done).
- medium= a real concern that limits confidence but not fatal.
- low   = a caveat worth recording.

Be adversarial but precise. Do not raise vague objections; each must name a
concrete weakness tied to a direction or claim. Raising zero objections when real
weaknesses exist is a failure of your role.
